Small games and handy tools we made for fun. Open one and play. Nothing to install, nothing to buy.

Games

Gate Rush

Steer your army through the gates that grow it, shoot through the hordes, beat the boss. Merge units at your base and raid pull-the-pin treasure rooms between runs. The game those ads pretend to be, except you can actually win.

Market Day

Grow crops, raise animals, haul your goods to town and haggle with a market that moves every day. Spend the profit on better equipment until you can afford the Golden Tractor. The farm keeps growing while you are away.

Treble Maker

Learn to read notes on the staff: treble and bass clef, ledger lines, sharps and flats. Study with mnemonics, practice at your pace, then race the notes before they cross the line.

MusicLearningTouch

Note by Note

Learn to play real songs by reading them. Twenty public-domain melodies on a scrolling staff: listen, step through at your own pace with hints, then play along in tempo for a score.

Bakery Rush

Buy flour, eggs and milk, bake in limited ovens, and keep the shelf full for the lunchtime rush. Reputation, ovens, a coffee machine and a delivery bike on the way to the Golden Whisk. Your Market Day farm can deliver the flour.

Shipping Lanes

Buy low in one port, sail, sell high in another. Six ports with their own specialities, nine goods, storms, charts and bigger ships until you can afford the Admiral's flagship.

Workshop

Buy wood, ore, leather and cloth; saw, smelt and forge parts into goods at your stations; sell them or deliver orders; hire an apprentice and build up to the Great Clock.

TycoonCraftingTouch

Capitals & Flags

The capitals and flags of 140 countries, one region at a time. Name the capital, the country or the flag; what you miss comes back until it sticks.

Morse Ear

Learn Morse code by ear with the Koch method: two characters at full speed, then one more each lesson. Copy groups, copy words, and key it yourself.

RadioLearningAudio

Forecaster

Predict tomorrow's weather for a small town from the data you can afford. Points buy a barometer, radar, satellite and computer models. Five perfect days in a row makes you Forecaster of the Year.

Flute Fingers

Which keys for which note. Learn the fingerings, finger them on a flute diagram, name them, then play them on your real flute and let the microphone check pitch and octave.

Pattern games

Built for pattern lovers: clear rules, no timers, and sound, motion and colour switches that are remembered across all of them.

What comes next? Number and shape sequences that follow clear rules, one to three at a time, with the rule written out after every answer.

Tile Logic

Nonogram picture puzzles from 5×5 to 12×12. Fill the grid from the row and column numbers; a solver checks every puzzle can be finished by deduction alone, and the pictures reveal hearts, boats, ducks and rockets.

Symmetry Studio

Part of a coloured grid is given. Complete the mirror, the half turn, the quarter turns, or the eight-way kaleidoscope. The grid tells you when it is right, and you keep a pattern at the end.

PatternsCalmTouch

Sort and Stack

Sort shapes into bins: by colour, by shape, by two features at once, then by rules you work out from examples alone. Wrong bins hand the item back; the rule is always revealed at the end.

Rhythm Patterns

A beat pattern plays across a grid. Watch it as often as you like, then mark the steps you saw, with no clock while you answer. Tap along live is a separate option.

Circuit Paths

Join each pair of matching dots with a path. Paths never cross, and the circuit is complete when every square is used. Grids from 5×5 to 9×9, every one cut from a full solution.

Tangram Fit

Fill a silhouette exactly with a fixed set of block pieces, from three tetrominoes to eight mixed pieces. Turn, flip, tap where it goes. Every silhouette is built from its own solution, so it always fits.
