Reference tone

Play the tone, then match it. Flutes usually tune to A at 440 or 442 depending on the group.

Tuning a flute

  • Warm up first: a cold flute plays flat and rises as it warms.
  • Sharp overall: pull the head joint out a little. Flat overall: push it in.
  • Rolling the flute in lowers the pitch; rolling it out raises it. Use this for single notes, not the whole instrument.
  • Soft playing tends flat, loud playing tends sharp. Tune at the dynamic you will play.
  • C♯5 and the third octave run sharp on most flutes; low notes often run flat. That is the instrument, not you.
