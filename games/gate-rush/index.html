How to play
  • Steer by dragging anywhere (or use ← → / A D).
  • Gates change your troop count. Green grows it, red shrinks it. Each gate shows what you would end up with.
  • Enemies fire back once you are close. A bigger squad wins fights with fewer losses. Side groups can be dodged; walls cannot.
  • The boss waits at the end. Arrive with as many troops as you can.
  • Coins buy permanent upgrades. Every level is beatable without them.
