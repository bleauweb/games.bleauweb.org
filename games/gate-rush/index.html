How to play
  • Steer by dragging anywhere (or use ← → / A D).
  • Gates change your army. Blue and gold grow it, red shrinks it, teal UPGRADEs every troop to the next tier, purple levels the hero. Each gate shows what you would get.
  • Hordes charge at you and fire back once you are close. A bigger, better army wins with fewer losses. Side groups can be dodged; walls cannot.
  • The boss waits at the end. If your power number is higher than its, you have it.
  • Base: merge two units of the same level to make a stronger one. Everything on the grid marches with you.
  • Treasure rooms: pull the pins in the right order. Water quenches lava, potions make the hero stronger, gold is gold.
